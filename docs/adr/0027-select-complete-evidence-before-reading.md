# Select complete evidence before reading

Status: accepted for implementation; release qualification remains required.

The owner authorized simplifying research and removing model verification. Reading a fixed allowance of lower-ranked provisions after discovery selection adds unrelated evidence, reference work and latency even for narrow questions. Initial discovery now selects a bounded set of potentially material provisions from authenticated titles and headings. The limit is a ceiling, not a target. Complete reading follows that selection and expands explicit same-instrument references using the existing authenticated lookup.

This supersedes the policy that discovery priority only reorders candidates before filling the read budget. A nonempty valid selection determines initial reads. Empty or unavailable selection retains deterministic bounded discovery. Unknown selected identities admit no evidence. Omitted candidates are recorded separately from read-budget and context-budget omissions. Both candidate lanes, release membership, complete provisions, temporal endpoints, source integrity, freshness and reference admission remain required.

Selection is a research hypothesis, not model verification of a Legal Answer. Headings cannot establish legal support or completeness. The writer receives complete admitted evidence, preserves known gaps and writes once; publication uses the programmatic checks in ADR0026. No domain-specific queries, authorities, answers or routing are encoded.

Fast initial planning expresses direct queries and underlying-rule queries in separate fields within the existing interpretation call. The latter investigates a disputed legal basis without assuming it applies; narrow lookups may leave it empty. Both groups share the topic inventory and are flattened only after shape, bounds and topic coverage validation. Invalid optional research does not discard a valid interpretation. This adds no planning call or automatic research loop.

Correctness, repeatability, answer delivery, retrieval deadlines and end-to-end latency still require qualification. Successful selection or a structurally valid answer does not prove those requirements.
