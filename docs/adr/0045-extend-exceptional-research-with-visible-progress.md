# Extend exceptional research with visible progress

Status: accepted.

Useful writing starts as soon as a supported finding is ready. Necessary research and reconciliation may continue for up to four minutes from submission through saved terminal delivery. This supersedes ADR0042's 60-second ceiling; sufficient evidence must trigger writing immediately, and extra headroom must not become an ordinary generation allowance.

The UI must visibly remain active throughout research, writing and saving, with truthful stage updates, periodic liveness updates and cancellation. A timeout or failed search establishes unfinished research, not that the corpus lacks the law; insufficient evidence with a partial answer is reserved for an established corpus coverage gap, and incomplete outcomes do not pass complete-answer qualification.
