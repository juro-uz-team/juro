# Qualify legal retrieval and answer completeness separately

Status: accepted.

Distinguish discovery of material law, complete reading, correct use of that law and saved delivery. A valid citation or successful retrieval does not establish that a Legal Answer preserved every material condition, exception or consequence. Keeping these responsibilities separate makes omissions attributable without adding a mandatory model assessment step to the answer path.

Runtime research remains driven by the user's question and available official law. Research notes and source identities support navigation and traceability; they do not replace complete provisions or establish legal entailment.
