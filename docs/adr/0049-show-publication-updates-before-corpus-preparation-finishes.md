# Show publication updates before corpus preparation finishes

Legislation Monitoring shows a Publication Update with its official link as soon as JURO observes it during discovery. The same entry opens the corresponding JURO edition once it is prepared and available in the activated corpus. This lets a reader consult a newly published decree while its scanned annex is being processed, and preserves the distinction between the time JURO saw the publication and the time its full text became searchable.
