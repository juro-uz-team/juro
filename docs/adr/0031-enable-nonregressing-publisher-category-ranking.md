# Enable nonregressing publisher category ranking

Status: accepted — 2026-10-20.

Preserve publisher classifications throughout the replacement corpus and enable their ranking contribution wherever paired qualification shows no retrieval-quality regression or material latency increase. A measured relevance improvement is welcome but is not required for launch. Where those conditions are not established, keep broad hybrid ranking and enable the category contribution only in qualified scopes.

Categories retain their original publisher scope and inheritance provenance; missing or unmatched classifications never exclude eligible evidence. Use the existing conservative latency allowance, at most the smaller of 10% or three seconds of increase within the absolute objectives, and protect known material qualifications. Unmeasured scopes do not qualify by assumption.
