# Prioritize grounded quality with Luna fast chat

Status: accepted.

Fast chat uses GPT-6 Luna and deep chat uses GPT-6.1 Terra. Research effort and orchestration may vary while preserving the model selected by the user's mode. This keeps mode selection predictable while allowing improvements to retrieval and writing.

Preserve relevant source-supported coverage, correct application and local citation support while reducing time before useful answer text appears. Stream substantive writing as it is generated, distinguish it from research progress, and identify the saved answer only after successful publication. Cancellation or failure must not present unfinished writing as a saved result.
