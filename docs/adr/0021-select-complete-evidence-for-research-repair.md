# Select complete evidence for research repair

Status: implemented for qualification; runtime acceptance remains required

Coverage assessment selects the complete provisions material to the question
from authenticated research results. It retains potentially material rules,
qualifications, exceptions, remedies and requested temporal endpoints. It may
exclude irrelevant background and redundant translations; it may not remove
an operative provision solely to meet a source count or latency target.

The initial search can fill the answer context before a missing rule is found.
Rejecting an entire subsequent packet when its union exceeds that limit prevents
productive repair. Research coverage assessment may therefore inspect the union
of the retained answer context and one newly authenticated packet: at most 48
sources and 128,000 characters of complete legal text. This extends ADR 0011's
assessment bound specifically for research coverage selection. Each incoming
packet still passes the existing authentication and context boundary separately.
The writer and independent verifier retain the 24-source / 64,000-character
answer limit. An oversized relevant set remains explicitly unresolved.

Selection accepts only supplied source identities. The server restores available
explicit same-instrument article dependencies transitively, including previously
excluded provisions, without crossing language, revision or temporal endpoints.
It preserves all authenticated identities for conflict detection within the
request. No provision is excerpted or truncated to fit. Semantic resolution of
a coverage gap requires evidence retained for the answer; selection cannot clear
operational failures. Repeated identical candidate sets reuse their selection
without inferring resolution of new gaps. The existing research-round budget
and independent claim, passage, dependency and publisher checks remain in force.

Fast research stops repair once coverage assessment identifies evidence for a
useful substantive answer to the requested decision,
preserving unresolved needs and recording the research limit. The writer may
answer only the supported portion, and the independent verifier still decides
which claims can be published. Related background alone cannot trigger this
stop. When indexed evidence cannot support the requested decision, Fast
still attempts official discovery and bounded recovery. Deep retains productive
repair across all three rounds. This refines ADR 0020's research policy for the
accepted Supported Partial Answer behavior and the Fast latency target; it does
not permit a research assessment to approve any public legal claim.

This removes an admission obstacle; it does not establish answer completeness,
adequate retrieval recall or acceptable latency. Those require live qualification
with failures included, followed by delivery, citation and persistence checks.
