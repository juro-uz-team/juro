# Separate research state from answer context

Status: refined by ADR0047; acquired law and original conversation remain available throughout the request.

Keep research state only where it serves continued research, avoids repeated work or preserves exact citation evidence; immutable corpus references can provide this without duplicating legal text. Reuse exact earlier context without duplicating identical legal text. Treat generated research requirements as provisional rather than allowing unnecessary expansions to block an answer to the user's actual question.

Complete acquired evidence, original conversation, exact saved citation associations and honest reporting of unexamined or missing law remain required. Removing request-local copies or redundant research records must not delete corpus legal data or reinterpret saved citations; no new persistent private-query logging is authorized.
