# Preserve qualified rules through answer publication

Status: accepted

Legal explanation and practical guidance share Qualified Rules rather than independently reconstructing their meaning or mechanically copying entire explanations into actions. Each public assertion must preserve the qualifications essential to its own meaning; the Main Point remains self-contained, and when its conclusion is unresolved it may explicitly lead supported analysis without asserting that conclusion.

Correction publishes the reviewed corrected answer rather than restoring an earlier whole answer because semantic retention mappings disagree. Revision-specific decisions and explicit unresolved issues preserve supported repairs while withholding unsupported content. Source identifiers, quotation matches and model approval are checks, not proofs of legal completeness.

These rules apply to arbitrary legal questions: production code must not encode fixture identities, particular legal answers, named-question routing or question-specific choices. Legal propositions come from authenticated official evidence and case facts. Offline regression tests may use synthetic rules and recorded failures, but do not establish fresh model-quality acceptance. The existing bounded writer/verifier/correction protocol, public compatibility and release acceptance remain in force.
