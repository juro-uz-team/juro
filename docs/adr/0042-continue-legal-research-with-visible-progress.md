# Continue legal research with visible progress

Status: the 60-second ceiling is superseded by ADR0045; ordinary responsiveness and truthful progress requirements remain.

Continued research must remain visible to the user. A responsiveness target miss must not force evidence loss or a falsely complete Legal Answer. Research, writing and saved delivery have distinct observable milestones.

Execution stops within the configured request deadline, or earlier on cancellation, terminal failure, exhausted admitted resources or repeated no progress. Extra time is headroom for necessary research, not permission to delay writing or accept routinely slow generation: write as soon as material evidence is sufficient, deliver promptly and diagnose research/writing time separately. Progress is distinct from final advice, and an incomplete result does not pass a case requiring a complete answer. Provider context and account/private-data boundaries remain enforceable without silently discarding retrieved law.
