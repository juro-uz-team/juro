# Trust the prepared corpus in legal chat

Status: accepted.

Legal chat treats the entire existing prepared Oracle corpus as having established integrity and authenticity. It consumes stored complete provisions and their existing provenance and citation artifacts without re-authentication, content-integrity testing or corpus re-certification during answering, chat qualification or chat release; this supersedes conflicting corpus-verification requirements in earlier decisions while preserving the stored corpus and its artifacts.

Corpus trust does not establish that a provision applies to a particular question or that a Legal Answer is materially complete. Selection happens after the question arrives through general corpus research, without question-specific provision lists or prepared answer plans. Application authentication, tenant isolation, correct association of generated citations with retrieved records, material legal reasoning, deadlines and atomic persistence remain distinct responsibilities.
