# Own research control and retained evidence coherently

Status: accepted.

One request controller owns bounded legal research, context capacity and an explicitly admitted terminal composition opportunity. A request-local workspace separates revisable Coverage Requirements, retained Evidence Inventory and the active complete Provision Set. A continuous research session retains acquired law across tool calls, with deterministic corpus acquisition and atomic publication behind small interfaces. Navigation and recovery limits must not strand acquired law or substitute identity checks for answer quality.

This refines ADR0040 and ADR0044 without reinstating fixed initial coverage plans. Initial material research questions retain their identifiers and remain revisable as the law clarifies relevance. Acquired law and prior conversation context remain available within the supported model window. An exact input count can reduce the required financial reservation; deployments may instead fund the complete model window. Neither admission path silently truncates context.

Every acquired complete source remains active and readable in the retained conversation, with its applicability metadata. Extracted navigation text is distinguished from a complete attached file. The model cannot remove evidence or earlier context. Repeated law and navigation metadata may reference their exact verbatim earlier occurrences; distinct law, metadata and provider history are preserved. These lossless references remove duplicate copies without summarizing or selecting away context. Context capacity exhaustion is an explicit unfinished outcome, not permission to select away law. Tool arguments bind acquired source handles and exact candidate/section pairs; invalid arguments receive bounded correction feedback while valid independent actions proceed.

Material research, including conflicting rules and applicability, precedes the Main Point. A dedicated writing tool may stream an individually supported Issue while independent research continues. Each public claim includes its material conditions and qualifications; source identities bind acquired complete law before prose reaches the browser. The Issue header supplies its canonical citation set; practical steps retain their own source subsets. Research tool arguments remain private.

After initial complete evidence arrives, the session first considers whether it supports a useful qualified Issue. It either publishes that Issue or privately defers writing with the missing basis before further research. Deferral never supplies public answer prose or removes acquired evidence, and this opening decision is not repeated after later revisions.

Later contradictory evidence requires an explicit, versioned correction or retraction with a visible explanation. Final assembly uses the exact latest Issue versions and preserves correction notes. If research cannot finish, previously shown Issues are explicitly withdrawn and that explanation is saved with the incomplete outcome. The Main Point is derived after reconciling the material issues. Provider deltas reach the application and browser directly; cancellation aborts outstanding work and waits for provider accounting cleanup. Only successful publication identifies a saved Legal Answer.

Before final composition, the configured model reviews each current Issue in a fresh input view containing the full original conversation, private case context, all acquired law and original source attachments. The view excludes the writer's internal assurances; those messages remain preserved in the writer history. Review output is private and records exact public claim spans, governing passages, material qualifications and conflicts. A valid defect verdict returns control to public correction or research rather than repeating review on the unchanged version. Review results rejoin the original writer history without replacing it. Reviews bind the exact published versions and acquired evidence; changed prose or law invalidates them. Needed changes still pass through the public revision tool. Retained-text quotations are checked against the acquired text. Quotations from attached source files carry page references and remain model-read evidence. These checks establish review provenance and freshness, not automatic legal entailment or completeness.

A missing material legal basis can request further research directly from reconciliation. Its requirement remains unresolved after narrowing or retracting an Issue. Confirmation requires acquired source content beyond the earlier evidence, quoted during review, and a revised public Issue. Removing an unsupported claim alone does not resolve the underlying research dependency.
