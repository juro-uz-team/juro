# Use targeted research for Legal Answers

Status: accepted for the replacement chat; current runtime transition pending

Legal Answer research searches the Indexed Official Corpus first and uses Live Official Search to fill missing official evidence. Secondary Web Research provides clearly attributed context when useful, without a mandatory wider-internet search on every live fallback. This replaces ADR 0001's mandatory secondary-research rule for the replacement chat: an unrelated research branch should not be required to complete an otherwise supported answer.

Legal rules, deadlines and mandatory actions require official evidence. When evidence is incomplete, the answer may retain useful supported portions and ask focused follow-up questions. Secondary material and model knowledge cannot substitute for missing official support. This decision changes request-level research policy; it does not change stored corpus evidence, embeddings, indexing or database contracts. Other provisions of ADR 0001 remain subject to explicit reconciliation before replacement implementation.
